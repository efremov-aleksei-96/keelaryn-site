Keelaryn

Keelaryn is a personal knowledge-management and automation system.

Google Drive integration

Keelaryn can connect to Google Drive after explicit authorization by the user. Drive access is used to operate the user's Drive-backed Keelaryn data repository.

Depending on the operation requested by the user, Keelaryn may read, create, update, move, or delete files and folders required by the Keelaryn workflow.

Privacy

Keelaryn does not sell Google user data or use Google user data for advertising.
